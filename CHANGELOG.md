### 1.0.0

First public release, following a hidden 0.1.0 Workshop upload.

- Seven scent moods: fresh, rotting and dessicated corpses (-2, -6, -1), fever (-2), infection (-4), spoiled food (-4) and the sickroom (-2, only with Communicable Diseases).
- Patches attach them to the matching vanilla, DLC and RimScent Extended sources; food poisoning reuses `RimScent_VomitScent`.
- Full French translation of the thought labels and descriptions.
- Requires RimScent and RimScent Extended; Communicable Diseases is optional.
